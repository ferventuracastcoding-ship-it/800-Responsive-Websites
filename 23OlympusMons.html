Explore the Mountain

A Mountain Beyond Earth

Olympus Mons is an enormous shield volcano located in the Tharsis volcanic region of Mars. Its enormous footprint stretches for roughly 600 kilometers, while its gentle slopes spread across the Martian landscape.

Anatomy of a Giant

01 / VOLCANO

Shield Volcano

Olympus Mons has broad, gently sloping sides formed by repeated eruptions of fluid lava.

02 / SUMMIT

Massive Caldera

At the summit is a huge complex of collapsed volcanic depressions created by repeated volcanic activity.

03 / THARSIS

Volcanic Region

Olympus Mons is part of Mars's enormous Tharsis volcanic region, home to several giant volcanoes.

Olympus Mons vs Everest

Olympus Mons is dramatically taller than Mount Everest when measured from its Martian base.

The Future of Mars Exploration

Olympus Mons is more than a spectacular geological feature. Studying Martian volcanoes helps scientists investigate the planet's volcanic history, interior processes, atmosphere, and evolution.
