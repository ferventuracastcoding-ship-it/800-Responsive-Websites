📖 Book of Life Minesweeper

🎮 How To Play

The Book of Life is a Minesweeper-style puzzle. Each hidden page contains either wisdom or a hidden trial.

🎯 Goal

  • Reveal all safe pages.
  • Avoid hidden ⚡ Trials.
  • Complete the entire Book of Life to win.

📖 Game Rules

  • 📖 = Safe page discovered
  • Numbers show how many nearby trials exist.
  • ⚡ = Hidden trial. Discovering one ends the journey.

🏆 Scoring

  • Safe page: +10 points
  • Trial discovered: -50 points
  • Complete the book: +500 bonus

💡 Strategy Tips

  • Use numbers to predict where trials are located.
  • Open empty areas to reveal multiple pages.
  • Plan carefully instead of clicking randomly.
