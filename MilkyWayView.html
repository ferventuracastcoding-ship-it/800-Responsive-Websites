Milky Way California

Explore California's darkest skies and discover the best places to see the galaxy.

Top 10 Milky Way Locations

1. Joshua Tree National Park

A world-famous desert destination with incredible Milky Way views, unique rock formations, and dark skies.

2. Death Valley

One of the darkest locations in California for astronomy and night photography.

3. Big Sur

Watch the Milky Way rise above the Pacific Ocean cliffs.

4. Lassen Volcanic National Park

High elevation landscapes with amazing star visibility.

5. Anza-Borrego Desert

A remote desert park perfect for astrophotography.

6. Yosemite

Granite mountains and star-filled skies create unforgettable views.

7. Sierra Nevada

Mountain wilderness with excellent dark sky conditions.

8. Mono Lake

Ancient lake scenery under the Milky Way.

9. Point Reyes

A coastal escape away from city lights.

10. Big Bear Lake

A popular Southern California stargazing location.
